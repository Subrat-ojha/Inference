## Scope

Operate-mode comparison surface for choosing a lively background treatment for the existing tracker header. The production header remains untouched until the user chooses.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Compare three truthful motion systems at the real header scale; refuse generic gradient decoration.

OWN-WORLD: Existing Study Ledger paper, ink, rules, mono measurements, square controls, and one-green state language.

STORY: Toggle theme and motion, compare each treatment, then choose the one that makes current progress feel alive without obscuring work.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact comparison controls followed by Signal Trace, Roadmap Pulse, and Model Pipeline headers using identical content and dimensions.

FORM: Direct local extension; user-fixed three-way comparison; seed not applicable.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
