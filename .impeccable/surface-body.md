# Inference roadmap tracker

- Primary target: `src/App.tsx`
- Mode: Operate
- Audience: one Java/backend engineer moving into production inference engineering.
- Job: select a week, complete proof-producing deliverables, record evidence and notes, and see honest readiness progress.
- Constraints: client-only persistence, responsive web, accessible controls, no account or backend.
- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/assigned.webp`

## Direction contract

THESIS: Progress is an evidence-bearing benchmark ledger, not a generic card dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Off-white run sheet, near-black ink, deep-green state marks, sage rules, square controls, tabular metrics, and almost no radius.

STORY: Scan all eight stages, open the current week, finish concrete work, attach proof, and watch credible readiness accumulate.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact title/progress strip sits over one continuous eight-row ledger. The active row expands inline into deliverables, proof, hours, and notes; surrounding weeks stay visible.

FORM: Benchmark result sheet, third grounded candidate, seed `b1f982a6`. Signature interaction: selecting a row relocates the expanded working sheet without changing page context.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
