# Free Hyderabad weekends

Originally researched 8 October 2026 and refreshed 17 October 2026. All times are IST. Standing preference: Saturday or Sunday in Hyderabad, with ₹0 admission. Free registration or host approval is acceptable; mandatory purchases and paid venue entry are excluded. Travel, parking, and optional purchases are separate.

## One simple plan for 10–11 October

- **Saturday, 09:30–16:30: QAlChemy Expo**, FINT Dallas Center, Raidurg. Register first; lunch and snacks are included. Bring a laptop for the afternoon workshop. [Organizer and agenda](https://qalchemy.fintinc.com/).
- **Sunday, 09:00–18:00: Manthan Samvaad**, Shilpakala Vedika. Register first; attendance and lunch are free. [Organizer and registration](https://www.manthansamvaad.com/).

These are suggested choices. Saved listings remain tentative, and saving does not register you. Pick an alternative below if you prefer a shorter session or a different topic.
